How to use this calculator

Choose the size, enter the numbers in the grid, and choose an operation. Answers are exact fractions where needed.

How it's calculated

2 × 2 determinant: ad − bc. 3 × 3: expand along the first row. Inverse = (1 ÷ det) × adjugate, where the adjugate is the transpose of the cofactor matrix. A matrix has an inverse only if its determinant is not 0.

Worked example

For [[1, 2], [3, 4]]: det = 1×4 − 2×3 = −2, and the inverse is [[−2, 1], [3/2, −1/2]].

Frequently asked questions

When does a matrix have no inverse?

When its determinant is 0. It is then called singular.

What is the adjugate?

The transpose of the matrix of cofactors. Dividing it by the determinant gives the inverse.
