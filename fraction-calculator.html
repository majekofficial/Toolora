How to use this calculator

Use fractions such as 3/4 and 2/3.

How it's calculated

a/b + c/d = (a×d + c×b) ÷ (b×d), then simplify by dividing by the greatest common divisor.

Worked example

1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6.

Frequently asked questions

Can the result be simplified?

Yes. Toolora reduces the fraction to its simplest form when possible.

What happens when I divide by zero?

The calculator rejects division by zero because it is undefined.
