Change the Subject of a Formula

Rearrange any formula, like v = u + at, to make a different letter the subject.

Calculate

Quick guide

  • Type a formula such as v = u + at.
  • Pick the letter that should become the subject.
  • Tap an example to see how it works.

How to use this calculator

Type your formula with an equals sign, then choose which letter you want on its own. The rearranged formula and every step appear immediately.

How it's calculated

To change the subject, do the same operation to both sides until your chosen letter is alone. When the letter appears squared, the quadratic formula or a square root is used.

Worked example

v = u + at, make t the subject. Subtract u from both sides: v − u = at. Divide both sides by a: t = (v − u) / a.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use any letters?

Yes. Use single letters. Letters next to each other multiply, so at means a × t. Use brackets for fractions, like (a+b)/c.

What if the letter is squared?

The calculator gives the ± square-root form, for example r = ±√(A / π).
