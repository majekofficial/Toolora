How to use this calculator

Fill in any three of the five values (s, u, v, a, t) and leave the other two empty. The calculator picks the right equations of motion, finds both missing values, and shows the working. Use negative numbers for slowing down.

How it's calculated

The equations of motion (SUVAT) for constant acceleration: v = u + at, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as, s = ½(u + v)t and s = vt − ½at². s is displacement, u initial velocity, v final velocity, a acceleration and t time.

Worked example

A car starts from rest (u = 0) and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 10 s. Then v = 0 + 2 × 10 = 20 m/s and s = 0 + ½ × 2 × 10² = 100 m.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I sometimes get two answers?

Equations with a square (like s = ut + ½at²) can have two solutions. For time, the negative one is left out. For velocity, the ± shows direction.

Which units should I use?

Metres, seconds, m/s and m/s². Keep them consistent.
