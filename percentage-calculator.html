How it's calculated

Worked example

15% of 850,000 = 850,000 × 15 ÷ 100 = 127,500.

Frequently asked questions

What is a percentage?

A percentage is a value expressed out of 100. For example, 25% means 25 out of 100.

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.
