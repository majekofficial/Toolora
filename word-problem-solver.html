How to use this calculator

Choose the type of problem (ages, consecutive numbers, sharing in a ratio, two travellers, work done together, rectangle perimeter, or items with a total cost). Enter the numbers from your question. The solver writes the equation, solves it step by step, and checks the answer.

How it's calculated

Each type is turned into an equation with a letter for the unknown, for example ages become kx + y = m(x + y). The equation is then solved exactly with the same engine as the Algebra Solver.

Worked example

A father is 4 times as old as his son. In 5 years he will be 3 times as old. Let the son be x: 4x + 5 = 3(x + 5), so x = 10. The son is 10 and the father is 40.
