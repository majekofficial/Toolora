How to use this calculator

Choose the formula (if there is more than one), choose what you want to find, then fill in the other values using the units shown. The calculator rearranges the formula, puts your numbers in, and shows every step.

How it's calculated

Lens and mirror formula: 1/f = 1/u + 1/v. Magnification m = v / u. Real objects and real images are taken as positive distances.

Worked example

An object 30 cm from a lens gives a real image 60 cm away. 1/f = 1/30 + 1/60 = 3/60, so f = 20 cm.

Frequently asked questions

What is a virtual image?

One that cannot be shown on a screen. With this sign convention it has a negative image distance.

Does this work for mirrors too?

Yes, the same formula applies.
