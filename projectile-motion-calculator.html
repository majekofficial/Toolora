How to use this calculator

Enter the launch speed, the angle above the horizontal, and optionally the starting height. You get the time of flight, range, maximum height and landing speed, with a graph of the path.

How it's calculated

uₓ = u cos θ and uᵧ = u sin θ. Time of flight t = (uᵧ + √(uᵧ² + 2gh₀)) / g. Range R = uₓ × t. Maximum height H = h₀ + uᵧ² / (2g). Air resistance is ignored.

Worked example

A ball is launched at 20 m/s at 45° from the ground (g = 9.8). t ≈ 2.89 s, range ≈ 40.82 m and maximum height ≈ 10.2 m.

Frequently asked questions

Which angle gives the longest range?

From level ground, 45° gives the greatest range.

Does it include air resistance?

No. Real paths are shorter because of air resistance.
