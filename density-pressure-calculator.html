How to use this calculator

Choose the formula (if there is more than one), choose what you want to find, then fill in the other values using the units shown. The calculator rearranges the formula, puts your numbers in, and shows every step.

How it's calculated

Density ρ = m / V. Pressure P = F / A. Pressure in a liquid P = ρgh. Upthrust U = ρgV (Archimedes' principle).

Worked example

Water (1000 kg/m³) at a depth of 5 m, using g = 10 m/s²: P = 1000 × 10 × 5 = 50,000 Pa.

Frequently asked questions

What is a pascal?

One newton per square metre (N/m²).

Why does pressure increase with depth?

There is more liquid above pressing down.
