How to use this calculator

Choose the formula (if there is more than one), choose what you want to find, then fill in the other values using the units shown. The calculator rearranges the formula, puts your numbers in, and shows every step.

How it's calculated

Newton's law of gravitation F = G m₁ m₂ / r², with G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg². Field strength g = GM / r².

Worked example

Earth (5.97 × 10²⁴ kg) and a 70 kg person at 6.37 × 10⁶ m from its centre: F ≈ 687 N.

Frequently asked questions

How do I type very large numbers?

Use e notation: 5.97e24 means 5.97 × 10²⁴.

Is r the distance to the surface?

No. It is the distance between the centres of the two masses.
