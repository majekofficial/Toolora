How to use this calculator

Choose what you want to do. You can simplify a square root, rationalise a denominator of the form a ÷ (b + c√d), or add and subtract surds.

How it's calculated

√(a × b) = √a × √b, so pull out perfect squares: √72 = √(36 × 2) = 6√2. To rationalise, multiply top and bottom by the conjugate: (b + c√d)(b − c√d) = b² − c²d.

Worked example

6 ÷ (2 + √3): multiply by (2 − √3) ÷ (2 − √3). The bottom is 4 − 3 = 1, so the answer is 12 − 6√3.

Frequently asked questions

What is a surd?

A root that cannot be written as a whole number or simple fraction, like √2 or √5.

What is the conjugate?

The same two terms with the sign between them changed: the conjugate of 2 + √3 is 2 − √3.
