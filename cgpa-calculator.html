How to use this calculator

Enter one semester per line using GPA,Credits. Example: 3.50,18

How it's calculated

CGPA = sum of (semester GPA × semester credits) ÷ total credits.

Worked example

3.50 on 18 credits and 3.80 on 20 credits: (63 + 76) ÷ 38 = 3.66.

Frequently asked questions

How is CGPA calculated?

The calculator weights each semester GPA by its credits and divides total quality points by total credits.

Can I use different semester credit loads?

Yes. Enter the actual credit load for each semester.
