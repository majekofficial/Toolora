How to use this calculator

Enter the total number of items n and the number you choose r. You get permutations (order matters), combinations (order does not matter) and factorials.

How it's calculated

P(n, r) = n! ÷ (n − r)! and C(n, r) = n! ÷ (r! × (n − r)!). Arrangements with repetition are nʳ.

Worked example

Choosing 3 people from 10: C(10, 3) = 120 teams, but P(10, 3) = 720 ways to give them first, second and third place.

Frequently asked questions

When do I use permutations?

When order matters, like race positions or passwords.

When do I use combinations?

When order does not matter, like picking a committee.
