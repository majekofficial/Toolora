About Toolora

A simpler way to get small answers.

Toolora is a free digital toolbox for everyday calculations, from maths and physics homework to health, time and money questions, with step-by-step working so you can understand every answer. We believe simple questions should have simple answers.

What we're building

Instead of making you search through complicated pages for one small calculation, Toolora puts useful tools in one fast, mobile-friendly place.

Our approach

  • Useful tools over filler content.
  • Clear interfaces over clutter.
  • Fast answers over unnecessary steps.
  • Transparent limitations when a tool depends on assumptions or user-provided data.

More tools are coming

We plan to expand Toolora based on what people actually need and use, not simply to create hundreds of pages.
