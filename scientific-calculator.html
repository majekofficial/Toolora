Quick guide

  • Switch between Deg and Rad for angles.
  • Use sin, cos, tan, ln, log, √, powers and n!.
  • Memory keys and Ans are built in.

How to use this calculator

Type or tap your calculation. Switch between degrees and radians with the Deg/Rad key. The answer updates as you type, and = saves it to your history. Ans inserts your last answer, and M+, M−, MR and MC work with the memory.

How it's calculated

sin, cos and tan use degrees or radians, and sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ give the angle back. ln is the natural logarithm (base e) and log is base 10. xʸ is a power, √ is a square root, and n! is the factorial of a whole number up to 400. Whole-number sums and powers are exact; other results are shown to 12 significant digits.

Worked example

In degrees, sin(30) = 0.5. √(16) × 2^3 = 32. 5! = 120. log(1000) = 3.

Frequently asked questions

How do I switch between degrees and radians?

Tap the Deg/Rad key. It starts in degrees, and Toolora remembers your choice.

What does Ans do?

Ans inserts the last answer you saved with =, so you can use it in the next calculation.
