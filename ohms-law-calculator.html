How it's calculated

Ohm's law V = IR. Power P = VI = I²R = V²/R. Charge Q = It. Energy E = VIt. Resistors in series: R = R₁ + R₂. In parallel: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂.

Worked example

A 12 V battery across a 4 Ω resistor: I = V / R = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 A.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the parallel total smaller?

Current has more paths to flow through, so the total resistance falls below the smallest resistor.

What is the unit of resistance?

The ohm, written Ω.
