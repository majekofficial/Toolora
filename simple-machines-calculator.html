How to use this calculator

Choose the formula (if there is more than one), choose what you want to find, then fill in the other values using the units shown. The calculator rearranges the formula, puts your numbers in, and shows every step.

How it's calculated

Mechanical advantage MA = load ÷ effort. Velocity ratio VR = distance moved by effort ÷ distance moved by load. Efficiency = (MA ÷ VR) × 100.

Worked example

A machine lifts a 400 N load with a 100 N effort (MA = 4). If VR = 5, efficiency = (4 ÷ 5) × 100 = 80%.

Frequently asked questions

Why is efficiency less than 100%?

Friction and the weight of moving parts waste some energy.

Examples of simple machines?

Levers, pulleys, inclined planes, wheel and axle, and gears.
