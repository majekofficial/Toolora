Slope Calculator

Slope, line equation, distance, midpoint and a graph from two points.

Quick guide

  • Enter the two points (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂).
  • Fractions and decimals are fine.
  • You get the line equation, angle and a graph.

How to use this calculator

Type the coordinates of two points. The calculator gives the slope, the equation of the line, the intercepts, the distance and midpoint between the points, and a graph.

How it's calculated

Slope m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁), which is rise over run. The line is y = mx + c where c = y₁ − m × x₁. The distance between the points is √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²) and the angle is the inverse tangent of m.

Worked example

Points (2, 1) and (6, 4): m = (4 − 1) / (6 − 2) = 3/4. Then c = 1 − (3/4)(2) = −1/2, so y = 3x/4 − 1/2.

Frequently asked questions

What if the line is vertical?

If x₁ = x₂ the bottom of the slope fraction is 0, so the slope is undefined and the line is x = a number.

What does a negative slope mean?

The line goes downhill from left to right.
