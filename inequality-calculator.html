Inequality Calculator

Solve linear and quadratic inequalities with interval notation and a graph.

Quick guide

  • Type your inequality with < > ≤ ≥.
  • Use one letter.
  • You get the solution, interval notation and a graph.

How to use this calculator

Type an inequality such as 2x + 3 ≤ 11 or x² − 5x + 6 > 0. The calculator solves it and shows the answer in inequality form and interval notation, with a graph.

How it's calculated

Solve like an equation, but flip the sign when you multiply or divide by a negative number. For quadratics, find where the expression equals 0, then test each region between those values.

Worked example

−3x > 12: divide by −3 and flip the sign, so x < −4. In interval notation that is (−∞, −4).

Frequently asked questions

Why does the sign flip?

Multiplying or dividing by a negative reverses the order of numbers, so 2 < 3 becomes −2 > −3.

What do the brackets mean?

A round bracket leaves the end number out. A square bracket includes it.
