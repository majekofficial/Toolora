How to use this calculator

Enter your list of numbers. The calculator sorts them and gives the mean, median, mode, range, quartiles, variance and standard deviation, with the working.

How it's calculated

Mean = sum ÷ count. Median = middle value of the sorted list. Mode = most frequent value. Variance (population) = Σ(x − mean)² ÷ n; sample variance divides by n − 1. Standard deviation = √variance.

Worked example

For 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9: mean = 40 ÷ 8 = 5, median = 4.5, mode = 4, population standard deviation = 2.

Frequently asked questions

Population or sample standard deviation?

Use population if your numbers are the whole group. Use sample if they are a small part of a bigger group.

What if there are two modes?

All the most frequent values are listed.
