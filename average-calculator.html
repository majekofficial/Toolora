How to use this calculator

Separate numbers with commas.

How it's calculated

Average = sum of all numbers ÷ how many numbers there are.

Worked example

For 10, 20, 30 and 40 the sum is 100, and 100 ÷ 4 = 25.

Frequently asked questions

What is an average?

The arithmetic mean is the sum of the values divided by the number of values.

Can I enter decimals?

Yes. Separate decimal values with commas.
