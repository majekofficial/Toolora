How to use this calculator

For adults, BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis.

How it's calculated

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². A BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 is considered the healthy range for adults. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis, so speak to a doctor about your health.

Worked example

70 kg at 1.75 m: 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.9, which is in the healthy range.

Frequently asked questions

What does BMI mean?

BMI is a screening measure based on height and weight. It does not diagnose health conditions.

Is BMI suitable for everyone?

BMI has limitations and is not interpreted the same way for children, pregnancy, athletes and some other groups.
