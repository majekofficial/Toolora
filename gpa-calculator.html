How it's calculated

GPA = sum of (grade points × credits) ÷ total credits, on a 5.0 scale: A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1, F=0.

Worked example

An A in a 3-credit course and a B in a 3-credit course: (5×3 + 4×3) ÷ 6 = 4.50.

Frequently asked questions

What GPA scale is used?

This version uses a 5.0 scale: A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1, F=0. Institutions may use different scales.

How is GPA calculated?

Each grade point is multiplied by its course credits, then total grade points are divided by total credits.
