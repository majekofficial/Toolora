How to use this calculator

Choose the formula (if there is more than one), choose what you want to find, then fill in the other values using the units shown. The calculator rearranges the formula, puts your numbers in, and shows every step.

How it's calculated

Wave speed v = fλ, where f is frequency and λ is wavelength. Frequency f = 1 / T, where T is the period.

Worked example

A wave with frequency 50 Hz and wavelength 2 m has speed 50 × 2 = 100 m/s.

Frequently asked questions

What is a hertz?

One wave per second.

What is the period?

The time for one complete wave, in seconds.
